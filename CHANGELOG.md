# Changelog

## v0.1.0-alpha

The first test build of ReRAC. It is an early alpha: you can walk around, but you can't play the game from start to
finish yet.

- For Macs with Apple Silicon only.
- You need your own copy of the game (the NTSC-U disc, SCUS-97199). No game files are included.
- Every planet loads, with its lighting, water and effects.
- Ratchet can run, jump and glide, and use the wrench and most of the weapons.
- The pause menu, the map and the Gadgetron vendor mostly work.
- Cutscenes play with their sound and music.
- Not yet: flying between planets, boss fights, the Clank sections, the main menu, saving and loading.
