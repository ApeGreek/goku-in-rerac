# Test request: Rilgar (2026-10-03)

`RC_LEVEL=5 cargo dev`.

### R1. The biters
- **Claim:** the small creatures idle and wander near home; when Ratchet comes into their area they run at him,
  weaving a little, stop short, glow brighter with a burst of sparks and snap at him. Their heads follow him. Light
  hits knock them back; heavy ones send them flying and they burst into pieces. Some only wake when Ratchet reaches a
  certain spot. They keep to their area and don't run through walls.

### R2. The flame tanks
- **Claim:** the tracked machines drive back and forth along their routes, treads rolling. When Ratchet comes close
  and in view, the turret turns to him and sprays flames that burn. They take a few hits, flash when hit, and blow up
  in three blasts and a shower of pieces.

### R3. The rising rocks
- **Claim:** rocks in the water sway, more when Ratchet stands on them. Nearby, one rumbles with bubbles and dust, then
  shoots up with Ratchet on it, floats and bobs for a while and sinks back with a splash sound. Standing under one as
  it rises pushes him aside.

### R4. The cars
- **Claim:** the cars ride between five stations on △; at the second station the route depends on the mission.
  Ratchet rides on them held in place.

### R5. The sewer
- **Claim:** going into the sewer changes the fog and tints the water; coming back out restores both.

### R6. Lifts, posts and riders
- **Claim:** the lift pads come when called and give a short cutaway ride. Hitting a post breaks it into falling
  pieces with sparkles. The riders loop high above the city with glowing ribbon trails.

### R7. The sea beasts
- **Claim:** beasts swim in the open water leaving wakes; in its spot, one lunges up out of the water and takes Ratchet.

### R8. The townsfolk
- **Claim:** bystanders turn their heads to watch Ratchet; the race girl, the bouncer and the salesman look at him as
  he talks to them.
