# Veldin's final boss 1422 with its arena (583, 586, 587, 644, 1906, the shots 564 / 624 / 628 / 983 / 1898) and Pokitaru's cutaway machine 1157

## 1. Summary

Ported in full from the decomp and the disassembly, registered by code identity in `units::PORTS`:

- **U363 1157, Pokitaru's cutaway machine** (level 11, #445 at (558.03, 557.04, 232.34), yaw 2.545; watches the floor
  switch 830 #366 at (546.49, 565.05, 232.75); camera cuboids 31 / 32). Builds 24 slats 1207, opens them in a cutaway
  (fade, Ratchet held in 0x72, script camera glide), state 5 at the end: the commando 114's phase 2 (his state 0xb) now
  completes and the O2 Mask hand-over is reachable.
- **U564 1422, the boss of level 18** (#875 at (498.41, 630.46, 102.55)): every state 0..0x1b, the hit intake and
  phase floors (350 health, sevenths), the Giant Clank round (body leave via `bodies::queue_leave`), the arena cuboids
  8 / 9 / 10 / 11, the attacks (shells 564, ring shells 624, beam 983, mines 568, hoppers 1906, the charge with aura
  628, the divers 1355), the pads / countdown, the death and finale (scenes 3 / 4 / 5, slideshow and ending movie
  requests, `PauseAllSounds(0x21)`), the HUD boss meter (slot 6), the class-18 camera record tweak, the glows, the jet
  puffs, the difficulty word 0x1623a8.
- **U557 644** the arena's cutaway director (#307); **U556 587** the floating platforms (28, groups 10 / 11 / 12);
  **U572 1906** the hoppers (18, group 21) with their glow and spawn-beam draws; **U554 583** the pads (#205..#208, group
  13); **U555 586** the countdown (#209) / floor button (#210); the shots **564 / 624 / 628 / 983 / 1898** (created only
  by 1422 / 983).

Shared layer extended (plug-and-play):
- `cinematic::set_fade(w, f)` — the gameplay fade 0x15f3fc (drawn by `rc-engine` scene_render's fade pass at
  `trunc(min(f, 1)·128)` black when no scene runs). `start_scene` clears it, and with Ratchet's health 0 calls
  `HeroCall::Death` instead of starting the scene (`DialogStreamStart`).
- `cinematic::focus_record(w, record, distance, pivot)` / `focus_suppress(w, record)` — a class's writes into a
  class-18 camera record (+0x34 / +0x38, and `0x306338`'s +0x50 = 1). `Services::camera_classes` / `camera_focus` mirror
  the level's camera slots for class code.
- `cinematic::enter_slideshow`, `play_movie_b(n)`, `pause_sounds(mask)` → `EngineRequest::{Slideshow, MovieB, PauseSounds}`
  (the engine logs them).
- `HeroFields::invulnerable` (0x13f510) and `HeroFields::back_request` (the back slot's request, 0x141414).
- `Services::death_z` (the level's death height 0x15f638, set by the engine).
- `creature::fx::jet_puffs` (L01 0x278810 / L18 0x266140) and `fx::puff23` (now shared with `cutscene_fx`).
- `Callback::UnitFrame(row)` + `units::frame_callback` (a unit draw callback's frame-side part: rand draws and 2-D
  text pushed into `DrawCallbacks::texts`, drawn by `rc-engine` subtitle_layer).
- `rolling_mine::throw` (0x2d5cf8), `falling_platform::start` + `group_command` (0x2f1c38 / 0x2fa888),
  `veldin_diver` reads the scene id from `svc.cinematic.scene`.
