# Test request: options, fly-bys, the grind race and polish (2026-10-04)

Commits 6920b8b..827f91f. Run with `cargo dev` (add `RC_AUDIO=0` only where sound is not under test).

### X1. Pause menu options
- **Claim:** every item on the original Options pages now changes the game while it runs: the camera's axes and speed,
  the Help Desk's voice and text, the subtitles in scenes, the music and effects volumes (effects heard at once,
  music on the sound page's preview too) and Stereo / Mono. The Controls page only shows the pad layout, as on the
  PS2: there is nothing to change on it.

### X2. Gold weapons
- **Claim:** with a weapon upgraded to gold (Gadgetron's second visit) it behaves as its gold version: the Bomb Glove's
  bigger blasts, the Devastator's extra blasts and retargeting, the Blaster's ricochets, the Mine Glove's homing
  mines, the gold Doom Blades, the Decoy's burst, the Suck Cannon's ten slots and the Morph-o-Ray's gold chickens.

### X3. The armed fly-bys (Orxon, Gemlik, Oltanis, Quartu)
- **Claim:** when one of these planets' scripted fly-bys plays, the screen goes black, comes back on a letterboxed
  camera flight with Ratchet held still, and fades back to the normal camera at the end. (Before this batch the
  fades played but the letterbox, the zoom and the hold did not reach the game.)

### X4. Oltanis's grind race
- **Claim:** grinding onto the three parallel rails near the bot lift starts a short letterboxed fly-by while Ratchet
  waits off to the side; it ends with him back on his rail at the start and the three rail bots right behind him.
  The camera then follows him around the race, swinging to the side and behind on the bends; early on Ratchet turns
  his head back to look at them; the bots fire their arcs at set points along the race (one at once, the others a
  little later). Falling off the rail hands the camera back to normal.

### X5. Giant Clank on Quartu
- **Claim:** as Giant Clank, inside the camera regions of the Quartu fight areas the camera slowly turns toward the
  area's focus point; moving the right stick stops it, and after a while of not touching it the camera turns back.

### X6. Blarg's boss
- **Claim:** after the boss on Blarg is beaten, the boss camera lets go and the follow camera returns.

### X7. Burns and the wrench
- **Claim:** on a burning floor the burn bounce throws a spark off Ratchet's side and puffs of fire while he rises;
  the burn death engulfs him in fire as he sinks; on Quartu sinking into lava flares up once. Swinging the wrench
  into a wall makes it ring and throw streaks and sparks from the spot it hit; the jump attack sparks where the wrench
  meets the ground.

### X8. Ratchet's touch and joints
- **Claim:** mines and other touch-reactive objects (Fleet mines, rolling mines, crates, Blarg troopers, Orxon
  brawlers) react when Ratchet walks into them, not only when hit. The Pokitaru and Kalebo characters turn their
  heads to Ratchet without load warnings; Hoven's gunners snap between their poses.
