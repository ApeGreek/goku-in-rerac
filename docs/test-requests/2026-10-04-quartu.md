# Test request: Quartu (2026-10-04)

`RC_LEVEL=15 cargo dev`.

### Q1. Guards and the Hologuise
- **Claim:** guards patrol or stand watch; seeing Ratchet they turn, raise the alarm (running to the alarm box and
  pressing it) and shoot from both arms, warning the others in sight. With the Hologuise on they stand down and walk
  back to their posts. Their eye lights pulse faster and redder when alerted.

### Q2. Flame drones and Giant Clank
- **Claim:** drones carrying a rider fly in and circle Ratchet, spraying flame streams; shot down, the rider tumbles
  off and the drone explodes. As Giant Clank, the jet robots fly in on their jets, fire volleys, lunge and swipe up
  close and jump between perches; buildings and walls crumble into burning bits and smoke when Giant Clank hits them.

### Q3. Electrified water, barriers and gates
- **Claim:** stepping into the electrified water shocks Ratchet with lightning and a white flash; sparks jump between
  points in the water; floor switches turn the current off for a while, with a countdown on screen. Energy barriers
  shimmer with drifting motes that gather where Ratchet touches them and switch off when their mission is done.
  Doors slide open as Ratchet approaches; Giant Clank's gates sink into the ground.

### Q4. Water and small things
- **Claim:** pools ripple with a gentle wave and scrolling texture. Bomb droppers drop bombs that explode on contact.
  The dispenser slides out its piece beside Ratchet.
