### F1. The two-way rails (582)
- **Claim:** where a rail is laid both ways, Ratchet always grinds it away from the end he jumps on at; jumping on
  from the other end takes the other direction. The rail he is on never flips under him. Two nanotech clusters wait
  near the rails and heal as usual.

### F2. The fields by the Trespasser locks (1392)
- **Claim:** beside each Trespasser lock a field hums and shows stacked green-to-orange bands that pulse outward,

### F3. The Hydrodisplacer pools (1402)
- **Claim:** each pool shows a scrolling water surface. Draining a full pool with the Hydrodisplacer lowers it (and
  what floats on it) smoothly; pouring into a drained one raises it back. Dying or reloading with a full gadget keeps
  the gadget full, and the pools start drained.

### F4. The boss's jet in the scenes (1799)
