# Test request: Novalis (level 01), batch C (2026-10-02)

`RC_LEVEL=1 cargo dev`, or play from Veldin.

### N1. Saves after the Infobot and gold weapons
- **Claim:** buying the Infobot from the Water Pump Worker saves the game (the save slot updates); buying a gold weapon
  from the vendor saves too.

### N2. Progress kept after a death
- **Claim:** after turning a bolt crank to the end (or lowering the bridge, or buying the Infobot) and reaching the
  checkpoint it gives, dying brings Ratchet back with that crank still done, the bridge still down and the Worker
  still gone.

### N3. Shot-down ships leave a burning wreck
- **Claim:** a Blarg flyer or a gunship hit hard enough explodes and sheds a burning chunk that tumbles away trailing
  smoke and a glow, then bursts where it lands or after about three seconds.

### N4. The Summoner mouse
- **Claim:** with the Sonic Summoner on, the little mouse that comes out of its house shows two glowing lights and
  puffs from its jets, and casts a small round shadow on the ground.

### N5. Small shadows
- **Claim:** the Drone Device's drones, the Mine Glove's mines and the Visibomb's missile cast small soft round shadows
  that shrink as they rise; the drones leave thin trails.
