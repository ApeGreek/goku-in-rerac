# Test request: Pokitaru (2026-10-03)

`RC_LEVEL=11 cargo dev`.

### P1. The invisible wall at the sea
- **Claim:** walking or swimming out towards the open sea, Ratchet is held back along a curve round the island and
  slides along it; he never gets out past it.

### P2. The tilting platforms
- **Claim:** the four platforms tip towards whichever side Ratchet stands on and swing back level when he leaves.
  Ratchet rides the tilt. Tipped far enough, they throw him off.

### P3. The Thruster-Pack buttons
- **Claim:** standing near a button without the Thruster-Pack, a hint asks for it; with it, a hint says to stomp.
  Stomping on a button pushes it down for good, and it stays down after leaving Pokitaru and coming back.

### P4. What the buttons work
- **Claim:** one button turns a piece 60° with a sound. The other starts a short scene: the screen fades, a machine
  unfolds in stages with clicks while the camera glides, and the screen fades back with Ratchet standing by the
  button. Seeing it again later, any button press skips to the end.

### P5. The pools
- **Claim:** the two pools in the caves have moving, layered water surfaces; up close a faint shimmer appears on them.
  The second pool's surface shows only from inside its cave or from high above.

### P6. The skill point
- **Claim:** destroying every member of the watched group earns a skill point, with the jingle and the banner.
