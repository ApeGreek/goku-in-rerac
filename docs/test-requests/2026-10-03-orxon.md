`RC_LEVEL=10 cargo dev`.

### O1. Lava and flames
- **Claim:** the lava spouts throw glowing rocks that bounce, cool and burst, and can be hit back. The flame vents
  burst on and off with a roar; their flames rise, spread along the floor and hurt Ratchet.

### O2. Barriers, wires and the core
- **Claim:** the energy barriers wiggle and hum and zap whatever touches them; destroying what a generator watches
  switches its barrier off and fades it away. The live wires run a buzzing charge with sparks that hurt. The
  generator core bursts into flames and rubble when hit.

### O3. Doors and platforms
- **Claim:** the cracked walls break from the front; the pressure plates hold their doors open while something stands
  on them; the lift, the sliding gate, the bridge and the sliding block move when triggered; the lava platforms sink
  under Ratchet and rise back.

### O4. Clank's section
- **Claim:** Clank's teleport pads send him to their partner with sparkles and the camera settles behind him; the
  pads pulse and send up rings. The gun drones rise when he comes near, fire at him and his gadgetbots, and fly
  away; destroyed, they break apart with bolts.

### O5. The air curtains
- **Claim:** the doorways to the open air shimmer faintly; walking through to the outside without the O2 Mask is
  where the air runs out, and coming back in gives it back.

### O6. The guard turret
- **Claim:** the turret turns to Ratchet and fires shells at him.
