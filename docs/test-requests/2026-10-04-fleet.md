# Test request: Drek's Fleet (2026-10-04)

`RC_LEVEL=17 cargo dev`.

### F1. Crew, lift pads and the shuttle
- **Claim:** crew members wander their area, spot Ratchet, chase and punch him; with the Hologuise on they salute
  instead. Lift pads glow softly and carry Ratchet between two heights, turning over so the Magneboots walk him onto
  the other face; a pad comes to fetch him when he waits at its far end. Standing on Ratchet's ship and pressing △
  flies him to the other landing spot with the camera following, the ship shrinking or growing and rolling over near
  the end; he is set down behind it.

### F2. Space, falling and mines
- **Claim:** two long glowing lanes stretch out into space and huge nebula squares slowly turn and breathe in the
  distance. Falling off the fleet into space still kills Ratchet, and flying the ship over it does not. Floating mines bob on the water and blow
  up with a splash when shot or touched.

### F3. Hints and skill points
- **Claim:** lingering at the energy barrier once the Hologuise is owned brings its hint; entering the water without the
  Hydro-Pack brings that hint; walking into the look area closes the help box, freezes Ratchet and turns the camera
  to one point, then another, before handing control back. Diving in the marked water and swimming out through the far
  end earns a skill point; downing every guard and crew member at the party earns another.
