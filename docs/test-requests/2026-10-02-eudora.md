# Test request: Eudora (2026-10-02)

`cargo dev`, Eudora.

### E1. The flying machines
- **Claim:** the small flying machines fly their routes with spinning rotors, some carrying a load underneath. Hitting
  one blows it apart into pieces; it comes back a while later once the camera is turned away. Destroying ten of them
  gives a skill point.

### E2. The brawler bots
- **Claim:** the bots wander near home or patrol, notice Ratchet, close in while keeping apart from each other, and
  the nearest one swings at him. A hit pushes one back; enough hits blow it up. Their engines hum and they puff
  exhaust when close to the camera.

### E3. The gunners
- **Claim:** the gunners turn to watch Ratchet, run off when he is far, dash about, fire bursts of three shots when in
  view, and strike when he gets close. A hit pushes or knocks them over; a kill throws them and they dissolve into a
  cloud of puffs.

### E4. The loggers
- **Claim:** the big loggers carrying a load growl and turn their heads toward Ratchet, start walking their path when
  he comes near, and swing when he is close. Hits make them flinch or stagger; a strong hit knocks them down; a kill
  throws them and they blow up into pieces.

### E5. The leg walkers
- **Claim:** the walkers stand at home (some chopping wood, throwing chips), turn toward Ratchet when he comes close,
  walk at him with their legs stepping on the ground, kick when next to him, and walk back home when he leaves their
  area. A hit knocks one down; it then dashes away from Ratchet.

### E6. Grabbable blocks
- **Claim:** Ratchet can hang from the edge of the blocks that offer a ledge.

### E7. The drifting speck and the cutscene thrusters
- **Claim:** near the speck's spot, a small speck appears now and then, slowly sinks and fades. In the cutscenes the
  infobot's thrusters fire.
