### B1. Rain, tanks, grenadiers
- **Claim:** it rains round the camera and leans with the wind, with splashes on the ground. The tanks break through
  their walls (or wait behind the crank's gate), patrol, turn their turrets and lob bouncing shells, and leave a
  smoking wreck. The grenadiers hide among their spots and lob grenades.

### B2. Ferries, bridges, small things
- **Claim:** the ferries carry Ratchet between the three docks. The bridges turn with their cranks. Steam, streaks
  and the scene thrusters show where they did before.

### B3. Bombers and burning wrecks
- **Claim:** the bombers fly in along their paths when Ratchet comes near and crash through their walls, which fly
  apart in pieces with an explosion. The wrecks burn with swaying flames, embers and sparks.

### B4. The turret battle
- **Claim:** Ratchet takes the anti-aircraft seat, aims and fires; the radar shows the gunships. The three kinds of
  gunship fly in, circle and attack in waves, firing missiles that climb and then dive at the turret. Shots knock
  pods and parts off them, which fall smoking and splash into the sea; enough hits bring the whole ship down. The
  turret's health shows on the HUD; winning or losing ends with the host.

### B5. The pools
- **Claim:** the two pools by the paths shimmer with scrolling water, brighter as Ratchet gets close.
