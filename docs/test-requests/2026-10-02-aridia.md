# Test request: Aridia (2026-10-02)

`cargo dev`, Aridia.

### A1. The anti-grav lifts
- **Claim:** standing on one of the glowing lifts carries Ratchet up or down its shaft with a spinning glow column and
  a hum; walking up to a lift at its other end calls it over. A lift stops short if Ratchet stands right under it.

### A2. The cannon in the distance
- **Claim:** the big cannon turns, fires a shell that flies off trailing smoke, and its boom is heard a moment later,
  later the further away Ratchet is.

### A3. Animated props
- **Claim:** the machinery around the level plays its idle animations at random intervals, some with a hum.

### A4. The gates
- **Claim:** the two-part gate stays shut until it is opened, then both halves swing open smoothly; on a later visit it
  is already open.

### A5. The boulder
- **Claim:** the large boulder only breaks from an explosion (not the wrench), bursting into dust and rock bits, and
  stays gone afterwards.

### A6. The arm platform
- **Claim:** after its mission, the platform on the big mechanical arm carries Ratchet between its two ends when he
  steps on, and comes to him when he waits at the other end.

### A7. The big path ships
- **Claim:** the big ships fly their routes with glowing lights and exhaust; hitting one breaks it apart, and the skill
  point for shooting the ships down still works.

### A8. The fire vents
- **Claim:** the vents spit flames with occasional bursts of sparks.

### A9. The launch tube
- **Claim:** after its mission, the tube's doors slide open and its pad rises at the end Ratchet walks up to. Standing
  on it, Ratchet walks to its centre, the pad sinks, the camera flies along the route to the other end and the pad
  rises there with Ratchet on it; he can then walk off. The first time in a while, a help message explains it.
