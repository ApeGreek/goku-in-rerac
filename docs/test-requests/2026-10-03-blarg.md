# Test request: Blarg (2026-10-03)

`RC_LEVEL=6 cargo dev`.

### B1. The crawlers
- **Claim:** the small goo creatures crawl over floors, walls and ceilings and come at Ratchet; some burst out of pods
  as he nears, some leap in from the edges. They splat into goo when killed, and the Suck Cannon can take them.

### B2. The troopers and the fire-wave bots
- **Claim:** the troopers wake as a group, close in round Ratchet from different sides, jab and back off, and keep to
  their area. The big bots slam the ground and send a wall of fire rolling out that follows the floor and stops at
  walls; up close they swing at him.

### B3. The mini-boss
- **Claim:** the boss drops in on a short cutaway, the doors shut and a health meter shows. It charges, rears up and
  slams, and rests now and then; at lower health a cutaway starts its next phase. Ratchet can't leave the arena
  until it's beaten.

### B4. Barriers, gates and glass
- **Claim:** the energy barriers crackle and hurt; their switches turn them off. The laser gates fade in as Ratchet
  nears, hurt on touch, and after a few touches a hint plays; they go down once their generators are done. The
  station windows show the glass with its shine, and the breakable window shatters into shards.

### B5. Clank's gadgetbots and the command menu
- **Claim:** as Clank, sleeping gadgetbots sit in glass bubbles that glow softly; hitting a bubble shatters it and
  the bot wakes and follows Clank, keeping close and stopping when he stops. They walk round walls rather than
  through them, wait at a closed door Clank went through, and hop back home if knocked away.
- **Claim:** holding △ as Clank opens his command ring (four commands round a circle, the stick or d-pad picks one,
  its name shows in the middle); releasing △ gives the order to the bots nearby. Follow (green glow), wait, attack
  (red: they share out the nearby creatures and strike them) and go to the pad (blue: they walk to the nearest pad
  and shrink into it; the pad counts down and opens its doors at zero). Before any bot has been near, the ring's icons
  show greyed and nothing can be picked.

### B6. The bridge, the launch tube and the escape
- **Claim:** after Clank's part the bridge extends piece by piece on a cutaway, then a hint and a save. The launch
  tube opens, and stepping in with △ shoots Ratchet up through it with sparks. The station's self-destruct starts
  the countdown on screen and wakes the later parts of the level.
