# Test request: Hoven (2026-10-03)

`RC_LEVEL=12 cargo dev`.

### H1. Burrowers
- **Claim:** the small creatures pop out of the ground in a spray of dust as Ratchet comes near, circle in sideways
  and bite; two hits kill one, a hit knocks it back. Some leap down from ledges to join in. The Suck Cannon can take
  them once they are out.

### H2. Gunners
- **Claim:** the soldiers wait out of sight, step out (some along a walkway) and close in sidestepping, then aim and
  fire single shots. Some smash the crates in their way.

### H3. Platforms, falls and arcs
- **Claim:** the hover platforms carry Ratchet along their routes or bob in place, tilting under his weight with jets
  blowing from their backs. The falls pulse and rise or drop when switched. The arc posts slide back and forth with
  crackling bolts between them that hurt.

### H4. Mines and dispensers
- **Claim:** the dispensers open as Ratchet approaches and release mines one by one; the mines pop up, roll at him
  swerving to the side and blow up on contact.

### H5. Helicopters and beams
- **Claim:** the helicopters circle overhead; shot down they explode with bolts and come back later out of sight;
  downing five earns a skill point. The power beams crackle until their generator is destroyed, then drop.
