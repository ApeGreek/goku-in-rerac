- **Claim:** rocks drift across the sky and meteors fall all over the level; they burst on the ground or on

  lava with dust.

### G2. Breakable rocks
- **Claim:** the rock piles crack apart a stage at a time when hit, spraying pieces; the last hit leaves rubble (and

### G3. Platforms and the raft
- **Claim:** the path platforms carry Ratchet between their two ends with their sounds; the lava raft rocks while it
  waits, sails its path when Ratchet stands on it (paddle wheel turning, a wake behind it) and can be called back
  from the far end.

### G4. The cannons
- **Claim:** standing in a cannon's seat mounts Ratchet; the stick aims it, ○ fires from alternate barrels with
  smoke and a kick, a crosshair shows in the middle, and △ gets off. Near an empty cannon it lowers, turns toward
  Ratchet and its seat light blinks on.
