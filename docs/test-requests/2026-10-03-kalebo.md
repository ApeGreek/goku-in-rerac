# Test request: Kalebo III (2026-10-03)

`RC_LEVEL=16 cargo dev`.

### K1. The arenas
- **Claim:** stepping into an arena shuts its barriers, the camera takes a fixed view that slides as Ratchet moves,
  and a second later the robots wake: some lob grenades that bounce, blink and blow up, some charge along a line and
  hurt on contact, some spray fire. Hits make them flinch; destroyed ones burst and drop back in from above while they
  have lives left. With all of them gone, Ratchet is set down outside, the camera returns and the barriers open.
  Ratchet can't leave an arena during the fight.

### K2. The cars
- **Claim:** once the cars are available, standing on one shows where it goes and △ sends it gliding off its station,
  along its route and into the next station, bobbing as it goes, with Ratchet riding and held on. The other car moves
  out of the way and reappears at the station just left.

### K3. The lifts
- **Claim:** a lift goes to the other end when Ratchet stands on it or waits at the other end; it hums and a column of
  spinning glowing rings shows while it moves; it stops rather than come down on Ratchet.

### K4. The grind rails
- **Claim:** leaning toward a red switch while grinding past it turns it green for good (hitting it does too); the
  gates on the rails pulse and click as Ratchet passes; grinding a set stretch without falling off earns a skill point.

### K5. The chicken pad
- **Claim:** near the pad, now and then its arms open, the beam rises and a chicken appears on it.

### K6. Scenery
- **Claim:** the shiny reflective surfaces shimmer as the camera moves; the big sign spins slowly; in the first scene
  two jets burn on the ship there.
